Range role: this is the **middle of §1** — the author's mod-`n^{4s+2}`
reduction of `P` (the `(n^s abck)^≤3` block of §F) down to `R ≡ 0 (mod n^{4s+2})`.

only outside facts it uses are (i) the numbered congruences (7)–(10) of p. 6,
(ii) the bổ đề-6 solution relations `h − b^n = a^n + 2n^s abck`,
`a^n + b^n = h − 2n^s abck`, `h − a^n = b^n + 2n^s abck` (p. 4/p. 6), and
(iii) the author's p. 9 R2 *`Quy ước`* that denominators `m(h−b^n)^r`
(`m ∈ {1,2,3,4,6,12}`, `r = 1..4`) are **not** cleared because they are
`≢ 0 (mod n)`.

Two design points the lane must fix before writing FRAG-08 (they are a
choice, not an ambiguity in the print):
1. **Home of the algebra.** `n^{4s+2}` is composite, so `ZMod (n^(4s+2))` is

   printed `=`-lines as identities in `ℚ` (or in a fraction field over the
   atoms `a, b, h, N`) and only then push them into `ZMod`, or (b) stay in `ℤ`
   and multiply every congruence by the common denominator, cancelling it by
   `IsCoprime`/`IsUnit`. Route (b) matches the author's own *`Quy ước`*
   (p. 9 R2) and the L7 patterns already in `ENCODING_MAP.md` §B.
2. **Atom set.** `set N : ℤ := (n : ℤ) ^ s * (a * b * c * k)` once per leaf;
