## Proposed leaf split for this range

Ids are **page-anchored** (`M1-FRAG-29A` …) so they cannot collide with the
sequential `M1-FRAG-01…` ids the pp. 6–23 scout wave emits — the same
convention `pp24-28.md` fixed; rename to the global source-order sequence at
assembly (this range is the one that follows pp. 24–28). A region listed without
an anchor is owned whole; an anchored region is shared at the quoted line.
